How it works

Three buttons, first thing on the screen.

No menus to dig through after a hard call. Each button opens the phone's own call or text screen, so the person always confirms before anything happens.

Reach a Chaplain

Call or text the department's designated chaplain. If no one is listed, a short form asks the chaplain to follow up.

Reach My Pastor

Each person saves their own pastor's name and number. It stays on their phone, and administrators can't see it.

Reach a Friend

A trusted friend, a spouse, or someone from another shift, one tap away. Saved only on their own phone, never on our servers.

The Pocket Chaplain helps first responders reach a real chaplain and the people they trust. It does not replace personal chaplain care, pastoral care, friendship, counseling, or emergency services.

About The Pocket Chaplain

A chaplain's ministry, in every pocket. Room for every faith, and for none.

The Pocket Chaplain was founded by Pastor Kendrick Meredith to support and extend the work of chaplains who serve police, fire, and EMS. It makes it easier to reach your chaplain and never stands in for a personal conversation. Each person can choose a faith preference, kept only on their own phone. The library includes Christian content and encouragement for people of all faiths, and content for other traditions is added by your chaplain team with input from leaders of those faiths.

Privacy

Built so every shift can trust it.

We collect only what is needed to provide the support someone asks for, and we're plain about who can see what.

What it isn'tTherapy, medical care, counseling, or emergency response. The app is not monitored for emergencies.

Questions

Common questions

Does this replace our chaplain?

No. The Pocket Chaplain exists to support and extend your chaplain's ministry. It makes it easier for your people to reach your chaplain, and it never stands in for a personal conversation.

Can command staff or the chief see who reached out?

Pastor and friend contacts, and any calls or texts made through them, never reach our servers. Prayer requests, follow-up requests, and ideas are visible only to authorized chaplain-team administrators, and people can send them anonymously.

Is it monitored for emergencies?

No. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. For a mental health crisis in the United States, call or text 988. Both appear on every screen of the app.

Do people need to download anything or make an account?

No. The Pocket Chaplain runs in the phone's web browser and can be added to the home screen like an app. Nobody creates an account.

What's different between the law enforcement and Fire & EMS versions?

The Reach buttons, privacy, and pricing are the same. Each version has its own prayers, reflections, scripture notes, and resources written for that service, and each department gets its own copy and data. Agencies that cover both police and fire can talk with us about a combined setup.

Does it work for volunteer and combination departments?

Yes. Licenses are priced by total people, career and volunteer alike. Volunteers often carry hard calls home without a crew around them, so having a chaplain one tap away matters even more.

Is it only for Christians?

No. Chaplaincy is for everyone. Each person can choose a faith preference on their own phone, and content for other traditions is added with input from leaders of those faiths.

How much work is it for our department?

Very little. We set up your copy. Your chaplain team updates contacts and content from a simple admin area whenever they like.

Are families included?

Yes. Every license covers all sworn, career, volunteer, and civilian staff and their families.
