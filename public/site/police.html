How it works

Three buttons, first thing on the screen.

No menus to dig through after a hard call. Each button opens the phone's own call or text screen, so the officer always confirms before anything happens.

Reach a Chaplain

Call or text the department's designated chaplain. If no one is listed, a short form asks the chaplain to follow up.

Reach My Pastor

Each officer saves their own pastor's name and number. It stays on their phone, and administrators can't see it.

Reach a Friend

A trusted friend, one tap away. Saved only on the officer's own phone, never on our servers.

Pocket Chaplain: Law Enforcement helps officers reach a real chaplain and the people they trust. It does not replace personal chaplain care, pastoral care, friendship, counseling, or emergency services.

Why it matters

The hardest part of the job often comes after the shift.

Officers see what most people never will, and many carry it quietly. Reaching out should take one tap, not a search through menus at two in the morning.

What's inside

Short, steady help for long shifts.

Everything is brief enough to use in a patrol car, a break room, or the driveway before walking in the door at home.

Take a moment

A one-minute guided pause: four slow breaths and an optional prayer. It respects reduced-motion settings, and it works without signing in.

Pocket Chaplain library

Searchable prayers, scripture, and reflections for stress and difficult calls, grief and loss, family strain, burnout, and recovery after a hard incident.

Private prayer requests

Send a request by name or anonymously. Choose who sees it: the chaplain team only, or an approved prayer group. Never posted publicly.

Share an Idea

A private way for officers and staff to suggest prayers, topics, resources, events, or improvements to the app.

Resources

Chaplain, peer support, grief, counseling, family, and crisis contacts, kept current by your department.

Your badge, your contacts

Each department gets its own copy with its badge, chaplain contacts, and local resources, managed from a simple admin area.

Works on any phone

Runs in the phone's browser and can be added to the home screen. No app store download and no account to create.

Daily scripture

A word for today, and a short study for the week.

A new verse every day and a four-day Bible study that moves forward on its own. Each day takes about five minutes. Scripture is from the King James Version.

Who it's for

For everyone who wears the badge, and everyone who waits up for them.

Officers & deputies

A quiet place to turn between calls, and a fast way to reach a chaplain or someone they trust before a hard week becomes a crisis.

Dispatchers & staff

Civilian staff hear and carry the same calls. Every agency license covers sworn and civilian staff alike.

Families

Spouses and children serve too. Families are included in every agency license at no extra cost.

Chaplaincy is for everyone

Room for every faith, and for none.

Each person can choose a faith preference, kept only on their own phone. The library includes Christian content and encouragement for people of all faiths. Content for other traditions is added by your chaplain team with input from leaders of those faiths.
